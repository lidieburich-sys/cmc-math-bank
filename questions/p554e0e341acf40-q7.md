## 题目

设 $`f`$ 在 $`[a,b]`$ 上连续。证明


```math
2\int_a^bf(x)\left(\int_x^bf(t)\,dt\right)dx
=\left(\int_a^bf(x)\,dx\right)^2.
```

<details>
<summary>展开参考解答</summary>

令 $`F(x)=\int_x^bf(t)\,dt`$，则 $`F'=-f,F(b)=0`$。所以


$`\displaystyle 2\int_a^bf(x)F(x)\,dx =-2\int_a^bF(x)F'(x)\,dx =F(a)^2-F(b)^2 =\left(\int_a^bf\right)^2.`$

</details>
