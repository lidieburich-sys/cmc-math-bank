## 题目

设 $`f(1)=0`$，$`f'(1)`$ 存在。求


```math
I=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f(\sin^2x+\cos x)\tan3x}{(e^{x^2}-1)\sin x}.
```

<details>
<summary>展开参考解答</summary>

因


$`\displaystyle \sin^2x+\cos x=1+\frac{x^2}2+o(x^2),`$


可导性给出 $`f(\sin^2x+\cos x)=\frac12 f'(1)x^2+o(x^2)`$。再用 $`\tan3x=3x+o(x)`$、$`(e^{x^2}-1)\sin x=x^3+o(x^3)`$，得


$`\displaystyle \boxed{I=\frac32f'(1)}.`$

</details>
