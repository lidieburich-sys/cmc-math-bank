## 题目

设 $`n`$ 为正整数，计算 

```math
I=\int_{e^{-2n\pi}}^1\left|\frac d{dx}\cos\left(\ln\frac1x\right)\right|\,dx.
```

<details>
<summary>展开参考解答</summary>

因为 $`\cos(\ln(1/x))=\cos(\ln x)`$，故


```math
I=\int_{e^{-2n\pi}}^1\frac{|\sin(\ln x)|}{x}\,dx.
```


令 $`t=\ln x`$，则


```math
I=\int_{-2n\pi}^0|\sin t|\,dt=n\int_0^{2\pi}|\sin t|\,dt=\boxed{4n}.
```

</details>
