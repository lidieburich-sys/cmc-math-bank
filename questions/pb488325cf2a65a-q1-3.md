## 题目

已知参数方程 $`x=\ln(1+e^{2t})`$、$`y=t-\arctan(e^t)`$，求 $`d^2y/dx^2`$。

<details>
<summary>展开参考解答</summary>

由


$$
\frac{dx}{dt}=\frac{2e^{2t}}{1+e^{2t}},\qquad \frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{e^{2t}-e^t+1}{1+e^{2t}},
$$


有


$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{e^{2t}-e^t+1}{2e^{2t}},\qquad \frac d{dt}\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)=\frac{e^t-2}{2e^{2t}}.
$$


故


$$
\boxed{\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=\frac{\frac d{dt}(dy/dx)}{dx/dt}=\frac{(e^t-2)(1+e^{2t})}{4e^{4t}}}.
$$

</details>
