## 题目

设 $`\Omega`$ 是由平面 $`x=0,x=y,y=z,z=1,z=2`$ 围成的空间区域，计算


```math
\iiint_\Omega\frac{dx\,dy\,dz}{\sqrt{y^2+z^2}}.
```

<details>
<summary>展开参考解答</summary>

区域可写为 $`1\le z\le2,\ 0\le y\le z,\ 0\le x\le y`$。故


```math
\begin{aligned}
I&=\int_1^2dz\int_0^zdy\int_0^y\frac{dx}{\sqrt{y^2+z^2}}\\
&=\int_1^2\left[\sqrt{y^2+z^2}\right]_{y=0}^{y=z}dz
=(\sqrt2-1)\int_1^2z\,dz=\boxed{\frac32(\sqrt2-1)}.
\end{aligned}
```

</details>
