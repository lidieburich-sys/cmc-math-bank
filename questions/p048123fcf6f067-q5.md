专题：[二重积分与三重积分](../topics/multiple.md)

## 题目

记


```math
D=\left\{(x,y):0\le x+y\le\frac\pi2,\quad0\le x-y\le\frac\pi2\right\},
```


计算 $`\displaystyle\iint_Dy\sin(x+y)\,dx\,dy`$。

<details>
<summary>展开参考解答</summary>

令 $`u=x+y,v=x-y`$，则


```math
x=\frac{u+v}{2},\qquad y=\frac{u-v}{2},\qquad
\left|\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}\right|=\frac12.
```


新区域为 $`0\le u,v\le\pi/2`$，所以


```math
\begin{aligned}
I&=\frac14\int_0^{\pi/2}\int_0^{\pi/2}(u-v)\sin u\,du\,dv\\
&=\frac14\left[\frac\pi2\int_0^{\pi/2}u\sin u\,du-\frac{\pi^2}{8}\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin u\,du\right]
=\boxed{\frac\pi8-\frac{\pi^2}{32}}.
\end{aligned}
```

</details>
